# MCP Tool Reference

When you connect Analytika as an MCP server to an AI assistant, the following tools are exposed automatically. The AI will select and call them based on your prompts.

## `list_websites`
Lists all websites you have tracked in Analytika, including their IDs, domains, and settings. Useful for querying a specific site.

## `get_realtime_visitors`
Returns the exact count and active page URLs of visitors online in the last 5 minutes.

**Example prompt**: *"How many people are on my site right now?"*

## `get_overview_metrics`
Returns high-level summary metrics: Unique Visitors, Pageviews, Revenue/MRR, Bounce Rate, and Avg. Session Duration over a chosen timeframe (`Today`, `Last 24h`, `7 Days`, `30 Days`, `YTD`).

**Example prompt**: *"Show me last week's summary stats."*

## `get_traffic_sources`
Returns the top acquisition channels, referring websites, search engines, and UTM marketing campaigns sorted by volume and conversions.

**Example prompt**: *"Where is my traffic coming from this month?"*

## `get_revenue_attribution`
Returns a revenue attribution breakdown showing which marketing channels, referrers, and landing pages generated paying customers.

**Example prompt**: *"Which traffic source drove the most revenue this week?"*

## `get_social_radar`
Returns discovered social mentions across X (Twitter) and Reddit for your domain, including community discussions and attributed referral traffic.

**Example prompt**: *"Who is talking about my product on Twitter right now?"*
