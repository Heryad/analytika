# Webflow Integration

Adding Analytika to your Webflow site only requires you to paste your tracking snippet into your site settings.

## Instructions

1. Log into your Webflow account and open your project.
2. Open the **Project Settings** (click the "W" icon in the top left).
3. Navigate to the **Custom Code** tab.
4. Under the **Head Code** section, paste your Analytika script:
   ```html
   <script defer src="https://analytika.me/a.js" data-website-id="YOUR-WEBSITE-ID"></script>
   ```
5. Click **Save Changes** and **Publish** your site.

## Verification

Visit your live Webflow site. The active visitors counter on your Analytika dashboard should immediately increment.
