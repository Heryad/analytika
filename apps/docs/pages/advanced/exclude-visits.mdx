# Exclude Visits

When you are actively developing your website, you probably don't want your own pageviews polluting your analytics data. Analytika provides several ways to exclude your own visits.

## 1. Localhost Exclusion (Default)

By default, the Analytika tracking script ignores any traffic coming from `localhost` or `127.0.0.1`. You do not need to configure anything to prevent local development traffic from appearing on your dashboard.

## 2. Using LocalStorage

If you want to exclude your visits on your live production site, you can set a flag in your browser's local storage.

Open your browser's Developer Tools (F12) while on your website, go to the Console, and run:

```javascript
localStorage.setItem('analytika_ignore', 'true');
```

As long as this flag is present in your browser, the tracking script will immediately abort and will not send any data to the Analytika edge servers.

## 3. IP Blocklisting

If you have a static office IP address and want to exclude your entire team, you can add your IP address to the blocklist in your Analytika dashboard under **Settings > Filtering**.
