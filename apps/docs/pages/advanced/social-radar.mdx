# Social Radar (𝕏 & Reddit)

Social Radar automatically discovers mentions of your website across **X (Twitter)** and **Reddit** and surfaces them directly in your dashboard — so you always know when people are talking about you.

## How it works

Analytika continuously monitors the web for any public posts or threads that mention your domain. When a mention is found, it appears in your Social Radar feed with the post content, author, and the number of visitors it drove to your site.

## Viewing Social Radar

1. Go to your Analytika dashboard.
2. Open the **Social Radar** panel.

You'll see a live feed of discovered posts from X and Reddit, sorted by recency and engagement. Each entry shows the post content, author handle, and attributed traffic.
