# Filter your Data

Analytika provides a powerful filtering engine to help you slice and dice your traffic data.

## How to use Filters

At the top of your dashboard, click the **Filter** button to add conditions. 

You can filter your dashboard by:
- **Date Range**: Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, Year to Date, Custom.
- **Location**: Country, Region, City.
- **Device**: Mobile, Desktop, Tablet.
- **Browser & OS**: Chrome, Safari, iOS, Windows, etc.
- **UTM Parameters**: Source, Medium, Campaign.
- **Custom Goals**: Only show users who triggered a specific goal (e.g., "Signed Up").

## Chaining Filters

You can chain multiple filters together. For example, you can filter by `Country = United States` AND `Device = Mobile` AND `utm_campaign = black_friday`.

The entire dashboard (including revenue, referrers, and live visitors) will immediately reflect the filtered segment.
